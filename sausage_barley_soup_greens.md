# Sausage and Barley Soup with Greens
_American_, _soup_, _pork_, _sausage_, _barley_, _one-pot_  
Preparation time: 10 min prep + 45 min cooking (55 min total)  
6–8 portions  

## Ingredients
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 225 g raw Italian pork or chicken sausage (casings removed)
* 1 medium onion, chopped (about 2 cups)
* 3 large carrots, peeled and chopped (about 2 cups)
* Salt and black pepper
* 1.4 L (6 cups) stock or water
* 1 can (400 g) diced tomatoes
* 100 g (½ cup) pearled barley
* 225 g (about 6 cups) tender greens, such as spinach or baby kale
* 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar or apple cider vinegar
* Grated Parmesan and fresh herbs (optional), for garnish

## Preparation
* Heat the oil in a medium Dutch oven or heavy pot over medium. Press the sausage into a thin layer over the bottom and cook undisturbed until golden underneath, about 5 minutes.
* Flip, break the sausage into bite-size pieces and brown all over, about 5 minutes more. If more than about 2 tablespoons of fat remain, pour off the excess.
* Add the onion and carrots, season with salt and pepper, and cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion is translucent, about 5 minutes.
* Stir in the stock, tomatoes with their juices and the barley, scraping up the browned bits. Bring to a simmer, then lower the heat and cook, stirring occasionally, until the barley is tender, about 30 minutes.

## Finishing
* When ready to serve, stir in the greens and cook until bright green and slightly wilted, about 2 minutes.
* Add the vinegar, then taste and adjust salt and pepper.
* Ladle into bowls and top with Parmesan and herbs, if using.

### Enjoy!

> **Tip:** With regular (hulled) barley, add about 15 minutes to the simmer.
> **Gluten-free:** Swap French green lentils for the barley.
> **Storage:** Keeps refrigerated up to 3 days. The barley absorbs liquid, so loosen leftovers with water or stock. If making ahead, add the greens only when serving.

---

Raw sausage (not precooked links) gives the best texture and flavour here. Barley adds chew, and a splash of vinegar at the end brightens the soup.
