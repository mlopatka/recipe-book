# Sheet-Pan Baked Feta with Broccolini, Tomatoes and Lemon
_American_, _Mediterranean_, _vegetarian_, _sheet-pan_, _oven_  
Preparation time: 25 min  
4 portions  

## Ingredients
* 1 bunch broccolini, ends trimmed and thick stalks split lengthwise (or broccoli, stalks trimmed and cut into bite-size pieces)
* 300 g (1 pint, about 2 cups) grape tomatoes, halved
* 1 small red onion, peeled, quartered and cut into 5 cm wedges
* 1 lemon: ½ cut into thin rounds, the other ½ left whole for serving
* 3 tablespoons olive oil, plus more for serving
* 1 teaspoon ground cumin
* ½ teaspoon red-pepper flakes
* Kosher salt and black pepper
* 2 blocks (170–225 g each) feta, cut into 2.5 cm slices
* Cooked orzo or farro, for serving
* 15 g (½ cup) fresh basil or cilantro leaves and fine stems, roughly chopped (optional)

## Preparation
* Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F) with a rack in the lower third.
* On a sheet pan, toss the broccolini, tomatoes, onion and lemon slices with the olive oil. Add the cumin and red-pepper flakes, season with salt and pepper, and toss again until evenly coated.
* Nestle the feta slices into the vegetables. It's fine if they break apart a little.

## Cooking
* Roast 15–20 minutes, stirring halfway but leaving the feta in place, until the broccolini tips are charred, the stems are easily pierced with a fork and the tomato skins blister and break down.
* Serve over orzo or farro. Drizzle with olive oil, squeeze over the remaining lemon half and top with fresh herbs, if using.

### Enjoy!

> **Tip:** Broccolini stalks are tender, so only trim the bottom 1 cm.
> **Variation:** Cut the broccolini, feta and lemon into bite-size pieces and toss with the orzo.

---

Baked feta turns creamy, like goat cheese but with feta's tang, while the broccolini crisps, the tomatoes burst and the lemon rinds soften.
