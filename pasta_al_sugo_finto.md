# Pasta al Sugo Finto
## Vegetable Ragu
_Italian_, _Tuscan_, _pasta_, _vegetarian_, _mushroom_, _simmer_  
Preparation time: 15 min prep + 55 min cooking (1 h 10 min total)  
4 portions  

## Ingredients
* 2 tablespoons plus 60 ml (¼ cup) extra-virgin olive oil
* 450 g button or baby bella mushrooms, cut into 1 cm pieces
* Salt and pepper
* 3 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 teaspoon dried oregano or rosemary
* 1 large white onion, finely chopped (about 2½ cups)
* 2 medium carrots, peeled and cut into 6 mm pieces (about 1⅓ cups)
* 2 celery stalks, cut into 6 mm pieces (about 1 heaping cup)
* 3 tablespoons tomato paste
* 240 ml (1 cup) dry red wine
* 680 g (24 oz) bottle tomato passata, or 800 g (28 oz) can tomato purée
* 450 g rigatoni
* Grated Parmesan (optional), for serving

### Pangrattato (optional)
* 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
* 30 g chopped walnuts
* 50 g bread crumbs
* ¼ teaspoon salt
* 2 teaspoons finely chopped fresh parsley

## Preparation
* In a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat, heat 2 tablespoons oil. Add the mushrooms, season generously with salt and pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned, 8–10 minutes.
* Add the garlic and oregano and cook until fragrant, about 2 minutes. Move to a bowl.
* Add the remaining 60 ml oil to the pot, then the onion, carrot and celery. Season generously and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and caramelized, about 10 minutes.
* Stir in the tomato paste and cook until fragrant, 1–2 minutes.
* Return the mushrooms to the pot and add the red wine. Cook until the pan is almost dry, about 5 minutes.
* Add the passata (or purée) plus 240 ml water swirled around the empty bottle or can.
* Bring to a bubble, then reduce to a simmer over medium-low and cook uncovered for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.

## Pangrattato
* While the sauce simmers, heat the oil in a medium pan over medium. Add the walnuts and cook, stirring, until fragrant and toasted, 2–3 minutes.
* Stir in the bread crumbs and salt and toast 2 more minutes. Off the heat, mix in the parsley and let cool.

## Finishing
* Cook the rigatoni in salted water until al dente. Drain, reserving 240 ml pasta water.
* Toss the pasta with the sauce, adding pasta water to loosen it until it clings. Season with salt and pepper.
* Serve topped with pangrattato and Parmesan, if using.

### Buon appetito!

> **Shortcut:** Shred the soffritto vegetables in a food processor instead of chopping by hand.

---

"Sugo finto" means "fake sauce": a meatless ragu from Tuscany and southern Italy where mushrooms and a soffritto (onion, carrot, celery) stand in for meat, in the spirit of _cucina povera_. The pangrattato turns it into a weekend dish.
