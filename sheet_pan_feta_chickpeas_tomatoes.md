# Sheet-Pan Feta with Chickpeas and Tomatoes
_Greek-inspired_, _American_, _vegetarian_, _sheet-pan_, _oven_, _meze_  
Preparation time: 40 min  
4 portions  

## Ingredients
* 3 cups cooked chickpeas (homemade, or two 425 g cans), drained, rinsed and shaken dry
* 450–570 g (2 pints) cherry or Sungold tomatoes
* 1 shallot, thinly sliced
* 60 ml (¼ cup) extra-virgin olive oil
* 2 tablespoons honey
* 1 teaspoon mild chile flakes (like gochugaru) or ½ teaspoon red-pepper flakes
* Salt
* 2 blocks (170–225 g each) feta, sliced 2.5 cm thick (sheep's or goat's milk, see Tip)

## Preparation
* Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
* On a sheet pan, stir together the chickpeas, tomatoes, shallot, olive oil, honey and chile flakes. Season with salt and spread in an even layer.
* Arrange the feta among the chickpeas.

## Cooking
* Roast, without stirring, until the feta and tomatoes are soft and the chickpeas are golden brown, 30–35 minutes.
* Eat right away. The feta hardens as it cools, so reheat any leftovers.

### Enjoy!

> **Tip:** Avoid cow's-milk feta. It doesn't have enough fat to withstand roasting.
> **Variations:** Good with red onion instead of shallot. Serve with tzatziki and naan, or with pita, grains, greens, hummus or yogurt. Swap the tomatoes for mini peppers, olives, dates or cauliflower, and the honey for anchovies, harissa, smoked paprika or turmeric.

---

Greek meze spreads often include a warm feta dish such as bouyiourdi (feta baked with tomato and hot peppers) or saganaki (fried cheese). This sheet-pan meal combines the two: salty, creamy feta against sweet tomatoes and honey-and-chile chickpeas.
