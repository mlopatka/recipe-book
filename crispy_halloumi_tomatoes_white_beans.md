# Crispy Halloumi with Tomatoes and White Beans
_American_, _Mediterranean_, _vegetarian_, _one-pan_, _broiler_  
Preparation time: 5 min prep + 25 min cooking (30 min total)  
4 portions  

## Ingredients
* Olive oil, as needed
* 450 g cherry or grape tomatoes, halved lengthwise
* 2 garlic cloves, minced or pressed
* 1 tablespoon finely chopped parsley, plus more for serving
* 1 teaspoon honey, plus more for serving
* ½ teaspoon dried oregano or thyme
* Salt and black pepper
* 1 can (425 g) cannellini, butter or navy beans, drained
* 225 g halloumi, cut into 6 mm slices
* ½ lemon
* Crusty bread (optional), for serving

## Preparation
* Set the broiler to high with a rack in the upper third of the oven, 8–10 cm from the heat.
* In a large ovenproof pan over medium heat, combine 2 tablespoons olive oil with the tomatoes, garlic, parsley, honey and oregano. Season with salt and pepper.
* Cook, stirring often, until the tomatoes soften and release their juices, about 10 minutes.
* Stir in the beans and heat through, about 3 minutes. Taste and adjust the seasoning, then turn off the heat.

## Broiling
* Lay the halloumi slices on top of the tomato-bean mixture and put the pan under the broiler until golden and crispy, about 5 minutes (depends on your broiler).
* Drizzle generously with olive oil, squeeze the lemon over the top and add a light drizzle of honey. Garnish with parsley and serve immediately, with bread if you like.

### Enjoy!

> **Tip:** Make a double batch when feeding four hungry people.
> **Swaps:** Any creamy canned white bean works; thyme can replace the oregano.

---

Halloumi turns golden and crisp outside under the broiler while staying soft and chewy inside, a good match for sweet, jammy tomatoes and creamy beans.
