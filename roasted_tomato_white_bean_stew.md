# Roasted Tomato and White Bean Stew
_Italian-American_, _stew_, _vegan_, _white-beans_, _oven_  
Preparation time: 30 min  
4–6 portions  

## Ingredients
* 25 g (½ cup) roughly chopped Italian parsley leaves and tender stems
* 2 teaspoons lemon zest (from 1 large lemon)
* 2 containers (about 280 g each) cherry or grape tomatoes
* 60 ml (¼ cup) olive oil, plus 2 tablespoons and more for drizzling
* 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves
* Kosher salt and black pepper
* 1 medium yellow onion, thinly sliced
* 3 large garlic cloves, thinly sliced
* ½ teaspoon red-pepper flakes
* 2 cans (425 g each) white beans (butter or cannellini), rinsed
* 360 ml (1½ cups) vegetable or chicken broth, or water
* Flaky salt (optional), for serving
* Toasted bread, for serving

## Preparation
* Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Toss the parsley and lemon zest together by hand and set aside.
* On a sheet pan or in a large baking dish, toss the tomatoes with 60 ml oil and the thyme. Season well with salt and pepper.
* Roast until the tomatoes collapse and turn golden at the edges, 20–25 minutes.

## Cooking
* Near the end of roasting, heat 2 tablespoons oil in a large (30 cm), deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium. Cook the onion, garlic and red-pepper flakes until the onion softens, 4–5 minutes.
* Add the beans and broth and bring to a simmer. Gently smash about ½ cup of the beans with the back of a spoon to thicken the broth (smash more for a thicker stew). Season with salt and pepper.
* Add the roasted tomatoes and all their juices. Simmer 5–10 minutes more, then season to taste.
* Ladle into shallow bowls. Top with the lemon-parsley mixture, a drizzle of olive oil and flaky salt. Serve with toasted bread.

### Enjoy!

> **Tip:** Great with garlic-rubbed toasted bread.
> **Variations:** Wilt in kale, escarole or Swiss chard at the end, add toasted bread crumbs, or top with grated pecorino or Parmesan (no longer vegan).

A pantry stew: tomatoes roasted in plenty of olive oil add sweetness and brightness to a rich onion-garlic-bean base.
